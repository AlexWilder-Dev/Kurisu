# Kurisu Omakase: research notes

Researched 19 October 2026. Everything below is sourced; anything uncertain is marked.

## Is it still running? Yes.

- **SevenRooms, live today:** the booking widget is taking reservations. There are seats on Tue 20, Wed 21, Tue 27, Wed 28 and Thu 29 October 2026. Every night from early November onwards shows request-only (full, or not yet released).
- **Google Maps:** 4.8 from 219 reviews. Reviews are dated "3 months ago" and "7 months ago". The listing reads "Located in: Ichiban" and "Opens 6 PM Tue".

## The room

- **Infatuation, 2024:** "a little, mahogany-clad room facing the pavement with an eight-person counter taking up 75% of the space. The counter, wooden and knee-high, is lovingly worn in".
- **Rayner, Observer, Aug 2021:** "The old tables where once we were served those Thai green curries, are pulled around to form a low counter." On the other side: "his brazier of glowing coals, his blow torch, sauces and, of course, his blonde-wood case of fish".
- **Brixton gets in.** Rayner: a bloke sticks his head through the door to greet Suwannee. Chris shrugs: "It's Brixton." Rayner adds blue lights and raucous laughter from the pavement. GSTP: passers-by "crane necks for a peek".
- **Ichiban's own photo of the room (4 Dec 2021):** grey walls and mint-green trim, framed geisha prints, a pink tree canvas, and a carved Thai figure by the window. Dark-wood tables are pushed into one long counter, laid with chopsticks on white paper, and a konro box and chopping board sit at the end.
- **Chris with the day's fish (Resy, 2023, courtesy of Kurisu):** he holds a blonde-wood box of fish. He wears a backwards black cap and a black tee with a mitsudomoe crest, and his forearms are tattooed. The art on the wall behind includes a samurai-helmet print.
- **Other details:** a chihuahua follows Suwannee about (Infatuation). There's no ginger or extra soy at the place settings (GSTP).

## How Chris talks and writes (the voice)

- **Spoken (Resy interview, 2023):**
  - "I want people to feel like they just left their friend's house showing off, rather than a restaurant."
  - "we tell jokes, we make them feel comfortable, we'll share stories about our journey to becoming Kurisu Omakase, some stories from my mum's restaurant".
  - On uniqueness: "the one thing we all have in common being humans is the fact that we're unique. So being able to showcase your uniqueness in whichever vessel you can, such as food, art, music — I think it's incredibly important."
  - On the best seats: "the two seats that are directly in front of my counter, where I do the chopping board … you get the best view from there, and then we smoke some of the sushi, so you really get the first few of those aromas". Then: "Though sometimes I tell people, these are the best seats." And: "Requesting specific seats is not really a thing. It's kind of like, first in, first service."
  - On the booking trick: go to the furthest month when dates go live, then add yourself to the notify lists for earlier ones. "When the guest told me that, I thought, 'you are a genius'. But now I said it, I think that trick is out the window now."
- **At the counter (Rayner):**
  - The temaki: "Here … Have a tuna spliff."
  - The scallop scored 18 times: "It's called the Houdini cut because it disappears on the palate."
  - Provenance comes with each piece: Exmoor caviar, Spanish bluefin, fish from Brixham.
- **Written (Instagram, newsletters):**
  - "To our beloved Friends and Guests"
  - "Nani? So all in what does this mean? Let's break it down:"
  - "Just a stupidly large and beautiful scallop from @hendersonseafood"
  - "It's been a fun, education and very very tasty learning experience"
  - "Arigato Gozaimasu 🍣❤️🙇‍♂️"
  - Hashtags: #BrixtonBaby, #PutFishOnRiceAndMakeItNice, #Shokunin
- **Character:** warm, informal, a little goofy, earnest about craft (ageing fish for 4–30 days), proud of Brixton. GSTP: "irreverent", and he insisted the last pour, a sweet pear sake, be taken as a shot.
- **Style name:** listings call it "Yoroppa-Mae" (Hot Dinners, SheerLuxe), a play on Edomae for European fish. GSTP: the nigiri "began with local produce … British fish from streams, seas and rivers".

## How the drop works (all sourced)

- **Dates drop by season.** Resy 2023: "we drop our dates four times a year for every upcoming season", "three months in advance". Summer 2025 opened June and July only.
- **How a drop is announced:**
  - Instagram posts ("*Note – Our next Omakase dates go live on the 27th of February @ 11PM on @resy").
  - Instagram Lives (GSTP: "an Instagram live, where Kurisu Omakase announced the moment they'd hit send").
  - The newsletter, with 30 minutes' early access (summer 2025: 4.30pm for subscribers, 5pm for everyone).
- **Speed:** it goes in seconds, or "within the hour" after Rayner's review (GSTP). Infatuation: "getting a reservation here makes a Glastonbury ticket feel like a copy of the …" (the end of the sentence is a link whose text I didn't capture).
- **Cancellations:** they go to a notify list, which emails within "five or 10 minutes" (Chris). Today SevenRooms has "priority alerts" (party size 1–8) and request-only slots.
- **Walk-ins:** "I'm afraid not". Only on the rare night someone cancels late.

## Booking terms, live on SevenRooms (October 2026)

- **Menu and price:** "We serve an 18 Course Omakase tasting menu led by Chef Chris with Family & Friends." "Our Signature Omakase is priced at £150 Per person."
- **Seatings:** two fixed slots, "1st Seating" at 18:00 and "2nd Seating" at 20:30. Each is 120 minutes and takes 8 covers. Parties of 1–8.
- **Days:** Tue, Wed, Thu and Fri (Sat, Sun and Mon have no shifts). **This settles the Harden's vs London-guide conflict in favour of Tue–Fri.**
- **Deposit:** "£25.00/Person – Deposit", taken at booking (`advanced_payment`, £25 per person, card required).
- **Cancellation:** "We ask that any cancellation to your reservation be made no less than 48 hours prior. For no shows or late cancellations, you will be subject to fee of £25 per person that will be applied to the credit card on file."
  - The confirmation blurb also says "let us know 24 hours before your reservation time". The 48-hour policy is the formal one, so we use 48.
- **Service charge:** SevenRooms default is 10%, which matches the brief's 10% service.
- **Booking notes, verbatim:**
  - "Please note our menu is to be served when all of our guests are present."
  - "Our Omakase menu will vary based on what we are able to source and receive from our wonderful suppliers each and every week."
  - "Our 18 courses will consist of various small hot and cold starters, followed by Nigiri sushi and then dessert."
  - "If you have any dietary requirements, please inform us on the next page. Unfortunately we may not be able to cater to these requirements on the day."
- **Dietary limits:** no vegetarian or vegan guests, no soy allergies, no guests who won't eat rice. This comes from the brief and the SquareMeal listing (seen in search; the page itself 403s). It is consistent with the SevenRooms note above.

## Who books, and why

- **Birthdays and special occasions.** The Infatuation tags it "Special Occasions", "Birthdays" and "Unique Dining Experiences". A Google review: "I went tonight with my girlfriend for her birthday".
- **Sushi obsessives and repeat guests.** GSTP's writer tried to visit monthly ("every single meal has been a new experience"). Rayner: "London's top sushi chefs are fighting to book" (Endo Kazutoshi, the Araki crew).
- **Locals.** Rayner lives 12 minutes away and ate at Ichiban for years with his kids.
- **Instagram followers.** 23.7K on Instagram, and that is where they find out about drops.

## What reviewers love

- The two of them together: "the duo's … legendary back-and-forth with guests" (Resy). "belly-laugh-inducing Chris" (Google). "the man is hilarious … ask for some of his wild 'home-o-kase' stories" (GSTP).
- The dishes:
  - The Houdini scallop.
  - Otoro "THC" (truffle, hanaho, caviar).
  - Crab croquette on nam jim ("A seriously f**king impressive ode to the Thai side").
  - Uni butter risotto.
  - Toro temaki, the "tuna spliff".
- Aged fish and provenance. The sense of theatre: "It is both dinner and theatre" (Rayner).
- The value compared with other London omakase (Rayner, Infatuation).

## What reviewers complain about

- **Getting a seat.** "It took me 6 months to get in" (GSTP). Rayner set an alarm. The Glastonbury comparison (Infatuation).
- **Late cancellations and churn.** Chris: "I look at one week and I know half of the guests, then I look at it again a few days later, and a lot of them have changed."
- **Price.** "expensive … a special occasion restaurant" (Infatuation). "pretty expensive but worth it" (Google).
- **Rice.** "slightly too wet and lacked the ideal bite" (Jason Leung, Substack, Apr 2025). He also calls the online reviews "quite polarising".

## What's missing because there's no site

- **No single place for the next drop date.** Today it is scattered across Instagram posts, Lives, stories and a newsletter whose signup sits three taps deep on a Linktree.
- **No public explanation of the notify list or the newsletter head start.**
- **The rules live only inside the SevenRooms checkout.** That covers the deposit, the 48-hour rule, "served when all guests are present", dietary limits and first-come seats. People meet them at the point of paying, and some never read them.
- **No story.** The 1999 restaurant, the 10-year-old waiter, the Tokyo detour, and the room handed from mum to son in 2021 are told only by journalists.
- **No answer to "can I just walk in?", "can I pick my seat?" or "do you do veggie?".**
- **kurisuomakase.com is parked.** The obvious URL leads nowhere.

## Design reasoning

- **Palette, from the logo:**
  - Text: ink brown `#42210b`, the logo's outline.
  - Accent: tuna red `#d33c4a`.
  - Highlights: skin peach `#fec1a6`, used sparingly.
  - Knife steel `#c8d8df` for quiet UI.
- **Palette, from the room:**
  - Night sections: mahogany `#3a1c13`.
  - Worn counter wood: `#987b4b` and `#c0a879`. The blonde fish box: `#e3c9a0`.
  - Coals: charcoal `#1c1d1a`.
  - Page: rice `#f7f1e6`.
  - Lacquer red `#8a0603`, from the osuimono bowl, for small moments.
- **Not used:** no gold, no black-and-gold, and no fake-Japanese type.
- **Type.** The logo has no lettering, so the type borrows its pen: a heavy, soft-cornered serif (Fraunces, SOFT axis up, weight 800–900). It reads like the same thick brown ink as the cartoon. Body text is **Karla**, the one typeface Kurisu has actually chosen (on their Linktree).
- **Layout.** Phone-first. It opens on the drop, not a hero image, because most visitors arrive from an Instagram post about dates. Cards wear the logo's 3px ink outline, like stickers.
- **Images.** The counter, room and food are always their own photos. Smoke, the torch flame and the 18 knife scores are drawn in SVG and motion.

## What the owners most want a site to do

1. One home for the drop. It should say when dates go live, link straight to SevenRooms, and explain the notify list and the newsletter head start, so Instagram Lives and DMs stop being the only way in.
2. Set expectations before anyone pays the £25 deposit: Tue–Fri, 6pm or 8.30pm, two hours, served when all eight are in, 48-hour cancellations, and no veggie, vegan, soy-allergy or rice-free guests. This should mean fewer no-shows and fewer repeat questions.
3. Tell the Chris-and-Suwannee story, and the room Ichiban handed over in 2021, in Chris's own register. Everyone who can't get a seat should go away holding the next date and the furthest-month trick, not a dead end. (Ichiban no longer trades, so it is not offered as a fallback.)
